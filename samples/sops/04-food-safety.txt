SUNRISE COFFEE CO. — FOOD SAFETY & HYGIENE SOP
Document ID: SOP-FS-004 | Version 1.0

PURPOSE
Protect customers and staff from foodborne illness.

HAND WASHING (REQUIRED)
Wash hands for 20 seconds with soap and warm water:
- Before starting shift
- After using restroom
- After handling raw food, trash, or money
- After coughing, sneezing, or touching face
- When switching between food and non-food tasks

GLOVE USE
- Single-use gloves required when handling ready-to-eat pastries.
- Change gloves every 30 minutes or when contaminated.
- Gloves are NOT a substitute for hand washing.

TEMPERATURE CONTROL
- Refrigerator: 38°F or below (check and log every 4 hours).
- Hot holding (if applicable): 140°F or above.
- Discard any dairy product past expiration date — no exceptions.
- Milk left at room temperature more than 2 hours must be discarded.

ALLERGEN AWARENESS
Common allergens in store: milk, wheat (gluten), eggs, tree nuts, soy.
- Allergen chart posted behind counter — reference before answering customer questions.
- If customer reports allergy: use clean utensils, new gloves, separate prep area.
- Never guarantee "allergen-free" — state "prepared with care, may contain traces."

ILLNESS POLICY
Employees must NOT work if they have:
- Fever, vomiting, or diarrhea (report to manager, stay home 24 hours after symptoms stop)
- Open wounds on hands (must be covered with bandage and glove)
- Contagious illness (cold with frequent coughing/sneezing)

PEST & SANITATION
- Report any pest sighting to manager immediately.
- Sanitize food contact surfaces every 2 hours with approved sanitizer.
- Store chemicals away from food prep areas.

INCIDENT REPORTING
Any customer illness complaint or foreign object in food: stop service of affected item, preserve sample if possible, notify manager within 15 minutes.
