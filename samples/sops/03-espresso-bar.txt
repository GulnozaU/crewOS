SUNRISE COFFEE CO. — ESPRESSO BAR OPERATIONS SOP
Document ID: SOP-ESP-003 | Version 1.0

PURPOSE
Standardize drink preparation for quality and consistency.

ESPRESSO EXTRACTION
- Dose: 18g coffee for double shot.
- Yield: 36g liquid in 25-30 seconds.
- If shot runs faster than 22 seconds: grind finer.
- If shot runs slower than 32 seconds: grind coarser.
- Discard any shot that blondes early or tastes sour/bitter.

MILK STEAMING
- Steam to 140°F for hot drinks (use thermometer until calibrated).
- Create microfoam: no large bubbles, glossy texture.
- Wipe steam wand immediately after every use.
- Purge steam wand for 2 seconds after wiping.

STANDARD RECIPES

Latte (12 oz):
  - 2 shots espresso
  - 10 oz steamed milk
  - Thin foam layer

Cappuccino (12 oz):
  - 2 shots espresso
  - 6 oz steamed milk
  - 4 oz dense foam

Americano (12 oz):
  - 2 shots espresso
  - 10 oz hot water

Iced Latte (16 oz):
  - 2 shots espresso (pulled into shaker)
  - Fill cup with ice
  - 8 oz cold milk

CLEANING (EVERY 2 HOURS)
- Backflush espresso machine with cleaning tablet (follow machine guide).
- Empty knock box.
- Sanitize steam wand tip.
- Wipe group heads and portafilters.

EQUIPMENT FAILURE
If espresso machine is down: offer drip coffee or cold brew. Notify manager. Do not serve espresso-based drinks until machine is repaired.
