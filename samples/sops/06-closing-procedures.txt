SUNRISE COFFEE CO. — CLOSING PROCEDURES SOP
Document ID: SOP-CLOSE-006 | Version 1.0

PURPOSE
Close the store safely, clean thoroughly, and secure the premises.

TIMING
Closing shift begins final cleanup 30 minutes before posted close time.
No new customers admitted after closing time (complete orders in progress only).

EQUIPMENT SHUTDOWN
1. Turn off espresso machine using proper cooldown procedure (not emergency off).
2. Empty and rinse portafilters. Leave portafilters out to air dry.
3. Clean steam wands, group heads, and drip trays.
4. Turn off grinders and blenders.
5. Shut down POS after final transaction is complete.

FOOD STORAGE
- Wrap all pastries. Label with date. Refrigerate perishable items.
- Discard any open dairy not used during shift.
- Wipe down all refrigerator handles and shelves.

CLEANING CHECKLIST
- Sweep and mop entire customer area and behind counter.
- Empty all trash bins. Replace liners.
- Sanitize all tables, chairs, and counter surfaces.
- Restock supplies for opening shift (leave note if running low).

CASH & POS
- Complete closing count per Cash Handling SOP (SOP-CASH-005).
- Run end-of-day report. Print and attach to deposit bag.
- Manager verifies and signs.

SECURITY (LAST EMPLOYEE OUT)
1. Verify all customers have left.
2. Walk perimeter — check restrooms, back room, storage.
3. Set alarm. Lock front door and back door.
4. Verify alarm armed (confirmation beep/light).
5. Do not re-enter after alarm is set without manager.

EMERGENCY
If alarm fails or door won't lock: call manager immediately. Do not leave premises unsecured.
