SUNRISE COFFEE CO. — OPENING PROCEDURES SOP
Document ID: SOP-OPEN-001 | Version 1.0

PURPOSE
Ensure the store opens safely, on time, and ready for customers.

RESPONSIBILITY
Opening shift lead (first employee on duty).

PROCEDURE

1. ARRIVAL (30 minutes before open)
   - Unlock front door. Disable alarm within 60 seconds using code provided by manager.
   - Turn on all interior lights and exterior signage.
   - Check voicemail and manager notes from previous night.

2. EQUIPMENT
   - Turn on espresso machine. Allow 20 minutes to reach operating temperature.
   - Turn on grinders, blenders, and POS system.
   - Verify refrigerator temperature is 38°F or below. Log reading on opening checklist.
   - If any equipment fails to start, call manager before opening to customers.

3. CASH DRAWER
   - Count starting cash: $200.00 in mixed bills and coins.
   - Record count on opening checklist. Sign and date.
   - Report any discrepancy over $2.00 to manager immediately.

4. PREP & CLEANLINESS
   - Wipe all counters, tables, and condiment stations.
   - Restock cups, lids, napkins, and stirrers at each station.
   - Brew first batch of drip coffee 15 minutes before open.
   - Display "Open" sign at 6:00 AM (or scheduled open time).

5. SAFETY CHECK
   - Verify fire extinguisher is accessible and gauge is in green zone.
   - Check that emergency exit paths are clear.
   - Confirm first aid kit is stocked.

NON-COMPLIANCE
Failure to complete opening checklist before serving customers may result in disciplinary action.
