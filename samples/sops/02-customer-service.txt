SUNRISE COFFEE CO. — CUSTOMER SERVICE STANDARDS SOP
Document ID: SOP-CS-002 | Version 1.0

PURPOSE
Deliver consistent, friendly service that builds customer loyalty.

THE 10-SECOND RULE
Every customer must be acknowledged within 10 seconds of entering the store.
Acknowledgment = eye contact, smile, and "Good morning!" or equivalent greeting.

ORDER TAKING
1. Greet the customer warmly.
2. Repeat the full order back before ringing it up.
3. Ask: "Would you like a pastry with that?" before 10:00 AM.
4. Offer dairy alternatives if customer orders milk-based drink: "We have oat, almond, and soy."
5. State total clearly and ask if order is for here or to go.

HANDLING COMPLAINTS
1. Listen without interrupting.
2. Apologize sincerely: "I'm sorry about that."
3. Offer solution: remake drink, refund, or manager callback.
4. If customer is angry or situation escalates, call manager immediately.
5. Log complaint in shift notes with time and resolution.

LOYALTY PROGRAM
- Ask every customer: "Are you in our rewards program?"
- New signups: enter phone number at POS, confirm SMS opt-in.
- Redeem rewards only after verifying account on POS screen.

UPSELLING (REQUIRED, NOT PUSHY)
- Before 10 AM: suggest pastry pairing.
- After 2 PM: suggest cold brew or iced options on hot days.
- Never upsell more than once per order.

DRESS CODE & PRESENTATION
- Clean uniform apron, name tag visible.
- Hair tied back if shoulder length or longer.
- No strong perfume or cologne.
