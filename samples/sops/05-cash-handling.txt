SUNRISE COFFEE CO. — CASH HANDLING & POS SOP
Document ID: SOP-CASH-005 | Version 1.0

PURPOSE
Prevent theft, errors, and ensure accurate financial records.

STARTING DRAWER
- Each register starts with exactly $200.00.
- Opening employee counts and signs checklist.
- Never share a register mid-shift without manager approval.

ACCEPTING PAYMENT
- Cash: count change back to customer aloud.
- Card: wait for "Approved" on screen before handing receipt.
- Mobile pay: confirm payment sound/notification before completing order.
- Never leave register drawer open unattended.

VOIDS & REFUNDS
- Voids under $10: shift lead may approve.
- Refunds over $10: manager approval required (call or text).
- Document reason on POS for every void/refund.
- Never void a transaction after customer has left without manager approval.

TIPS
- Tip jar is for all staff — collected and split per payroll policy.
- Do not solicit tips aggressively.
- Card tips processed through POS only.

MID-SHIFT DROP
- If drawer exceeds $400, perform cash drop into safe envelope.
- Record drop amount on checklist. Manager verifies at end of day.

CLOSING COUNT
- Count all cash, compare to POS report.
- Acceptable variance: ± $3.00.
- Variance over $3.00: recount with second employee, notify manager.
- Deposit bag sealed and placed in safe. Only manager handles bank deposit.

SECURITY
- Never discuss alarm codes or safe combinations in public areas.
- Two employees required for safe access after closing.
- Report suspicious behavior to manager immediately.
