COFFEE SHOP STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES

Opening Procedures:
1. Arrive 30 minutes before opening.
2. Turn on espresso machine and allow 20 minutes to warm up.
3. Check milk expiration dates and discard expired products.
4. Count cash drawer and verify starting balance of $200.

Customer Service:
- Greet every customer within 10 seconds of entering.
- Repeat orders back to confirm accuracy.
- Offer a pastry with every coffee order before 10am.

Closing Procedures:
1. Clean all equipment including grinder and steam wand.
2. Empty and sanitize all drip trays.
3. Lock front door and set alarm code 4521.
